FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need To Know

Clear answers to common questions about bookings, flights, cabs, and payments.

How far in advance should I book South India packages and cabs from Madurai? +
We recommend booking 1 to 2 weeks in advance for festive temple seasons (like Chithirai or Rameswaram darshans) and peak hill station months (April-June) to ensure your preferred vehicle and top resort rooms.
Do you provide company-owned vehicles with experienced drivers? +
Yes! Sri Komban Travels operates a 100% company-owned fleet (Innova Crysta, XUV 3XO, Force Urbania, Force Traveller Maharaja) with seasoned, background-verified chauffeurs experienced with South Indian hill and highway routes.
Can we hire cabs only without booking full tour packages? +
Absolutely. Our dedicated vehicle fleet (Mahindra XUV 3XO, Toyota Innova Crysta, Force Urbania Luxury Vans, and AC Buses) is available on simple per-kilometer or daily rental packages for local Madurai trips and outstation routes.
What payment options are available? +
We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, NEFT/RTGS, and all major Credit/Debit Cards. Trips can typically be confirmed with a nominal token advance, with the balance payable in convenient stages.
